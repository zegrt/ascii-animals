## The fix

There's no drawing fix: the card's look and rules (t9, d1) are unchanged. What changed is the evidence, and the
preview's procedure:
- **The drawn check** (`WhatsNewCard.watchPreview`, preview only): 1.5 s after the card opens (after its 0.45 s fade
  and 0.4 s content animation), the test copy reads its card window's image from the window server (falling back
  to `cacheDisplay` if that fails, and saying which), saves it as **`build/energy-test/card-drawn.png`** (confined to
  the test folder), and logs `energy-card: pid=<p> launch=<id> drawn=<share> size=<w>x<h>`.
  `WhatsNew.drawnShare` counts pixels with alpha above 8 of 255.
- **Why the card went:** every close names its reason, and the preview logs
  `energy-card: pid=<p> launch=<id> closed=<click|own-click|esc|try|keep|screens|meadow|welcome> after=<s>s`.
- **`energy.sh preview`:** opens the test copy with `open -g` (in the background), so the director's app keeps the keyboard
  and the answer needs no click. It says "don't click anywhere until you have looked", waits up to 10 s for the drawn
  line and **fails before asking** when the share is under 0.5, the line is missing, or the card closed first. After
  the answer it prints whether the card was still up, or why and when it closed.
- **`--snapshot-whatsnew-drawn <png>`:** the same measurement on the real card, off every display. With
  `ASCII_ANIMALS_SNAPSHOT_BLANK=1` it hides the content after the fade-in, standing in for the reported failure.
  `ASCII_ANIMALS_SNAPSHOT_ICON_HIDDEN=1` gives the card without the arrow. It warns when the displays sleep or the
  screen is locked.

**Drawn share, before and after** (the probe, the card's code otherwise unchanged; displays awake):
- before (8d310ca's card code, measured with the experiment probe at 531e636): **0.999** without the arrow, **0.977**
  with it;
- after (this branch, the release test copy from a6df509, `--snapshot-whatsnew-drawn`, displays awake):
  **`drawn=0.977 size=460x333`**; with the icon hidden (no arrow) **`drawn=0.999 size=460x325`**;
- an empty card (`ASCII_ANIMALS_SNAPSHOT_BLANK=1`): **`drawn=0.000 size=460x333`**, which energy.sh fails ("the
  card drew 0.000 of its window (460x333 points): empty").
- Bitmap: **`build/energy-test/card-drawn.png`** (the whole card: plate, arrow, title with its cursor, line, both
  chips, "esc close"). Its preview area is the live view's dark plate: off every display the Meadow's display link
  doesn't tick, so it presents no frames. On a display it ticks (~52 a second) and draws, which the "frames by hand"
  and "on the built-in" runs measured. The empty card's image: `build/energy-test/card-drawn-blank.png`. The live
  preview rewrites `card-drawn.png` with what the card holds on screen.

## Rulings

- **The cause is reported as "likely closed by a click", not fixed as a drawing bug.** I didn't invent a card change
  for a fault I couldn't reproduce. If wrong (the full app does blank the card), the live preview's drawn check
  fails before the director is asked, and its saved image shows what the window held. Cost: one more preview.
- **Windows on the owner's display, without them present** (concern 2). (a) One probe ran the real card with only its
  top-right 1 x 1 pt (transparent: the corner above the card's body) on the built-in's bottom-left corner, for 3 s,
  click-through. (b) A scratch tool put 2 x 2 pt red panels for about 1 s at the built-in's bottom-left and just
  under its menu bar at x 1400, with a zero-width status item. Neither took focus, raised a dialog or touched the
  owner's apps or settings, and nothing was the app's desktop. I judged them inside "never a live desktop run"
  because they were snapshot or scratch runs with at most a few red points showing. The committed probe stays off
  every display.
- **The drawn threshold is 0.5** of the window: a whole card is about 0.98 (its rounded corners and the room beside
  its arrow are transparent) and an empty one is 0. A half-drawn card fails too.
- **The live drawn check runs at 1.5 s** after the card opens. A click before then fails the preview as "closed
  before its drawn check", which is the right answer: the director was asked not to click.
- **`open -g` for the preview only.** Measurement and calibration launches are unchanged. In calibration the director
  must click the fish icon anyway.

## Noticed

- The README's "The Web costs no more than the Ocean" comes from 3.0's numbers. In 3.1's session the Web's app CPU is
  higher than the Ocean's at 30 fps (13.7 % against 11.5 %) and equal at 15. I kept the sentence (no new claims); it
  may want a look.
- The card's own typing and cursor timers (`typer`, `blinker`) and the presenter's once-a-second look are in the
  default run-loop mode, so they pause while a menu is open. That's harmless today (a click on the menu closes the
  card), but it's the same pattern as the readings timer.
- The window server draws no window while the displays sleep or the screen is locked, so any window-image check
  needs a seeable screen. The live card only opens on one; the probe now says so.
- Upgraders meet the same rule the preview hit: the card comes up after an automatic update and the first click
  anywhere closes it. That is d1's design. Whether it gets enough time on screen is a design question for the
  director, not something to change here.
