## Rebuild

Commits: 62bc678 proof (the brief's five new checks only), then the rebuild and this report.

**The model** (`Sources/OceanKit/Domain/WallpaperMemory.swift`). A file in our Stills folder is ours; anything else is
the person's. Each display gets two files per covered wallpaper, `<display>-<code>-A.png` and `-B.png`; a refresh
writes the one not showing on the Space in view (`nextStill`), then sets it. `<code>` is 16 hex digits of a hash of
the wallpaper's URL and options, and `covered` maps it back (a small table; no per-still records). When the Space in
view shows something outside our folders, it becomes that display's wallpaper (`own`). A restore reads what the Space
in view shows and puts back what its file names; 3.0 stills (`still-…` in our folder) and ASCII Sharks stills (its
folder) go back to the original saved for that display (3.0's `originals`, kept as `saved`, never changed; Sharks'
own memory or the hand-over copy). Nothing is ever deleted: the keeper has no list or remove call any more. A failed
read does nothing (no set, no restore, no memory change); the next event tries again. Stills are written beside the
file and renamed over it, so a hidden Space never shows half a picture (`SystemWallpaper.write`).
**When things happen** (main.swift and the menu, unchanged since fix round 1): with matching off, the Space in view is
restored at launch, on a Space switch (a visit), a wake and a display change; quit restores whether matching is on
or off; turning matching off restores; Restore Original Wallpaper restores once. **Uninstall** restores the Space in
view, keeps the Stills folder whenever it holds a file (or can't be read) and says: "If a desktop still shows the
animals, choose your wallpaper in System Settings, then delete this folder: ~/Library/Application Support/ASCII
Animals/Stills." It no longer writes the memory back after removing the settings. **Deleted:** the per-still
journal, adoption, cleanup, the restored set, `memoryIsOurCopy` and the uninstall keep-or-discard decision (the app and
OceanKit are about 110 lines shorter). The Settings line stays exactly "Your own wallpaper comes back when you
quit or turn this off."

**Folder size.** At 3600 × 2338 a still is about 0.33 MB (Ocean), 0.54 MB (Web), 0.71 MB (Meadow) (review-r2-r1).
3.0 kept the newest 3 per display (up to about 2.1 MB) and deleted older ones, which could blank a hidden Space;
bb6df14 grew by one per refresh (100–214 MB a month at 10 a day). Now: 2 per display per covered wallpaper, about
1.4 MB per display for the usual one wallpaper, and 1.4 MB more for each other wallpaper the habitat covers (another
Space's picture, or a new pick while matching is on). It never grows with refreshes. 3.0 and Sharks stills stay as
they were (3.0's at most 3 per display).
