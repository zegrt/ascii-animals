> - 🕸 **Pick a habitat.** The **Ocean**, with sharks and fish; the **Web**, with spiders spinning orb webs, flies wandering by and a jumping spider on the hunt; or the **Meadow**, with butterflies drifting over a field of flowers.

> - 💦 **Click empty desktop.** In the Ocean, a splash. In the Web, a fly drops in, and if it lands in a web, the spider comes for it. In the Meadow, a flower blooms.

> - 🦈 **Choose your animals.** Up to eight sharks from eight species (hammerhead, tiger, mako, thresher and more); a line-up of spiders: the classic six, or all garden spiders, wasp spiders or golden orb weavers; or butterflies: monarchs, blue morphos, swallowtails and cabbage whites, and how many of them.

## Rulings

- **The GIF is 8 s and 5.2 MB**, against the Web hero's 16 s and 2.7 MB. Seven butterflies beat their wings in
  separate places, so most of each frame changes; 16 s came to 9.8 MB, and fewer colours lost the coneflowers' purple.
  8 s matches the Ocean hero's length. If wrong: a slower page load on GitHub; a shorter loop or gifsicle's lossy mode
  (not installed here) would cut it.
- **The Meadow GIF has no pointer.** `frames --interact` scripts only the Web, and the brief allows a tool flag only if
  media needs one; a plain run shows the habitat well. If wrong: a scripted Meadow run (stir, land, bloom) would be a
  new OceanTool flag.
- **Hero layout:** Ocean and Web stay side by side at 430 px; the Meadow gets its own centred row at the same width
  (three across would shrink all three GIFs to under 300 px).
- **README wording "and how many of them"**, not "as many as you like", since How many stops at 12.
- **About says "XERO WORKS' own"** (the brief's words) rather than "our own": in a window, "our" has no clear owner.
  The README and CHANGELOG say "our own", in the voice they already use.
- **The sketch is linked by commit** (`blob/4e1b869/…`), not by branch, so the link holds if the branch moves.
