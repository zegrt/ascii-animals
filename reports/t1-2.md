## Rulings

1. **Commit order.** All the strengthened and new checks went in first, alone (4b8ad72): the red step. Then one
   commit per finding. If that's wrong, it costs nothing: the history just reads differently.
2. **`flowerSeekersReachLowHeads` setup changed after its red commit** (in ae769b8). As first written, it aimed at
   a head at x = 104 from up to 1600 pt away. 15 s at foraging speed (80 pt/s) covers only ~1200 pt, so it failed
   on distance whatever the walls did. Now:
   - it aims at the 1-row head nearest the middle, from up to ~600 pt away;
   - the test itself is unchanged (within half a cell);
   - it still fails without the fix (monarch 22.4 pt) and passes with it.

   If wrong: the reviewer may want the starts farther away and a longer flight.
3. **Finding 4(a): the check's shape.** "Every upstroke flight is the same flight as no lift at all" is stronger
   than the review's "vertical force ≤ gravity". The "frozen stroke sinks" half skips the morpho. Its bound tilts
   the wanted airspeed up 22°, and research §1.4's "drag toward the wanted airspeed" carries that, so a frozen
   morpho in a climbing bound holds its height (0.3 pt sink in 1 s). Every other species sinks > 30 pt. If
   wrong: lift on the upstroke would still fail the check, but a drag that lifts the body wouldn't.
4. **Finding 4(b): a new species value, `surge`**, the σ of the wanted speed, redrawn at every clap (est.; not in
   the research's parameters table).
   - The white is 0.45, with the pace held to 0.4–1.6× its speed, so 56–224 pt/s against a cruise of 140.
   - This took a sweep: at 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3 the white came out at 3.2, 1.75 and 1.3.
   - Basis: a real white changes speed about 30 % within a beat to reach 8.5 m/s² along the path, and its
     free-flight speed is 2.37 ± 0.44 m/s.
   - The other species are 0, so they fly exactly as before (no extra random draws).

   Cost if wrong: the white's pace visibly lurches fast and slow. That is one number to retune, and worth the
   director's eye at T5's first still, next to finding 9.
5. **Finding 3 covers any target**, not only `.approach`, so `cursor` landing gets it too. A target outside the
   terrain gets no wall margin at all; `butterfliesStayInside` and the reach check's inside test still hold.
6. **Finding 7 also scales the travel speeds**: how fast the weather's noise pattern (200 pt/s) and its gust
   fronts (450 pt/s) cross the screen. They are speeds given at 13-pt cells too. With them scaled, a meadow on
   small cells gets exactly the same weather, slower. Pointer writes are the user's real pointer and stay
   unscaled.
7. **Finding 2's cap uses the rect the butterfly is in** (`rectIndex(near:)`). Thresholds for "not banded" are
   mine: middle third ≤ 70 %, top ≥ 5 %, bottom ≥ 10 %, 6 of 9 cells.
8. **Finding 1's undo.** It is an allocation-free snapshot of the flight state, taken after every finite step. A
   restore also drops the waypoint, so a fresh one is picked.

## Noticed

1. **CI hasn't built this branch.** Finding 6 is done by the review's list and the local flag, but only CI's
   Swift 6.3.3 can confirm it. The board already says: before approval.
2. **Finding 8 changes what a caller must pass.** The flower ages against the land's clock, so `bloom(time:)`
   must be given the land's time; T3's world should pass `land.time`. A caller passing another clock gets a flower
   born at the wrong age. For example, `time: 0` after 10 s gives a fully grown flower straight away. Worth a line
   in T3's brief, next to finding 11.
3. **The monarch's glide ratio is still true by construction** (review 4(a)'s side remark): a glide sets the
   airspeed onto the glide slope, so `flightPathsMatchMeasurements` reads back 1/tan 15.5°. The fix list didn't
   include it.
4. **`fly` costs a little more per step:** 0.20–0.25 µs now, against the review's 0.13–0.18. That is
   `rectIndex(near:)` every step, plus the clearance loop over the edges in targeted flight. Still negligible.
5. **`Butterfly.scale` is still set by no caller.** The world must set it from the cell (finding 7's second half,
   routed to t3).
