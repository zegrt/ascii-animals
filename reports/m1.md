## What was built

- **MeadowTuning:** every slider default and constant m1 uses, named as in the sketch, each with its `html:N` line
  at f20b833. That covers the seven kinds, the rosette and floret recipes as data, and the port's own rules,
  each marked as the port's with the reason. The instance holds the director's slider values. `sanitized()` follows
  the sketch's `normalize`: a value that isn't a number keeps the default, one out of range is clamped.
- **MeadowMath:** the sketch's code ported exactly:
  - `mulberry32`, and the two streams (`seed` and `seed ^ 0x5bd1e995`);
  - `R`, `RI`, `gauss` and the seeded Fisher–Yates shuffle;
  - `hash3`, `vnoise`, `n1`, `smooth`, and `easeOutBack` (overshoot 1.4).

  The check compares golden values made by the sketch's own functions under node. All of them match bit for bit,
  except `gauss`, which is within 1e-12.
- **Breeze:**
  - the turning wind, base speed and two-octave gust bands rolling downwind;
  - the 48·S stir grid and the downwash grid, stored as Float like the sketch's Float32Arrays, decaying at
    exp(−1.6·dt);
  - `stir` (radius 3, (1−d)²·0.16, ±260) and `wash` (±200);
  - `downstroke` (z < 0.35 only) and `flutter` (the swallowtail on a flower);
  - the three sums in `MeadowAir` (all air, landed, pointer alone).

  The pointer is fed once a step through the sketch's `stirStep` (see the first ruling).
- **Flowers:**
  - `make`, which draws in the sketch's exact order and takes an id for every candidate, kept or not, as the sketch
    does;
  - `plant`, per screen of a `Terrain`;
  - `step`: lift, fade, growth, the visited dim, and the head spring at ζ 0.12, capped at 0.45 R;
  - `nod`, `visited`, `setBusy` and `setFeeding` for the world;
  - `click`: touch the nearest bloom, else bloom only where there's room, else touch the bloom in the way. A touch
    nods the head, makes a puff and fires `onTouched`. Then the click limit applies, with the oldest flower closing
    and fading over 2 s through `onClosing`;
  - `relayout` and `replant` (the sketch's one replant transition, ending in `onReplanted`).

  Flowers are found by id only. Steps run at a fixed 1/60 s, and nothing allocates once warmed (checked by counting
  real heap allocations).

How each brief check is held:

- **Same seed, same meadow and air; the fixture matches.**
  - `meadowSameSeedSameMeadow`: two screens, a pointer and clicks, run twice. The flowers and air are identical,
    and another seed differs.
  - `meadowReplaysTheSketchFixture` replays `air-flowers.json` with:
    - the seed, settings and viewport from the file;
    - the trace fed as the sketch's handlers take it: each move is recorded, a press and release under 6 pt
      clicks, and leaving ends the pointer;
    - exactly 1/60 s a tick, never the file's rounded `dt` (re-check 2's R2-1).

    At ticks 0, 1, 60, 600 and 1800 it compares every wind point (all, landed and stir), and every flower's id,
    kind, centre, radius, head and age. They agree to the file's 6-decimal rounding.
  - `meadowReplaysTheSketchProbe` does the same for my two earlier probes, at 1600 × 913 and at 1280 × 713 (a
    cell of 10, S 0.77). It also compares the petals, seeds, rosettes, florets, rotations, f0 and the pollen count.
- **Stir half-life ≈ 0.43 s and stays local; the same air at 15–120 Hz within 5 %.**
  - Half-life: after 26 steps (0.433 s), 0.50 ± 0.01 of a stir remains.
  - Locality: nothing reaches past 4 grid spacings (3 cells of reach plus 1 cell of interpolation).
  - Input rates: the same 2 s gesture, at 300–900 pt/s, is fed several ways: at 15, 30 and 120 Hz regularly; in
    bursts (60 Hz in fours, 120 Hz in sixes); and jittered (60 Hz and 30 Hz, each sample 0–3 steps late). The
    pointer's stir is compared over a 25 × 15 grid every 0.25 s for 3 s, against 60 Hz. The worst gap is 0.8 %
    (limit 5 %). The 30 Hz total also matches the 60 Hz total within 5 %, so nothing is lost when two steps' worth
    arrives together.
  - The sketch's own rule fails the same test (printed, not checked): 10.8 % at 30 Hz, 33 % at 15 Hz.
- **Downwash only under z < 0.35:**
  - nothing at z ≥ 0.35 or on the upstroke;
  - more lower down (z 0 against z 0.34);
  - the push points outward;
  - it isn't counted in the landed air or the pointer's.
- **Wind at breeze 1.7 peaks in the sketch's range:** 90.9 pt/s over an hour, checked within 88–100. See Concerns.
- **No flower centre in a gap; no overlap below 1.05; patch count follows area and density:**
  - Gaps: 24 seeds at density 3 on three two-screen layouts (offset displays, a gap between displays, and the
    owner's laptop under the monitor). No centre is in a gap, and none is more than 10 pt past a real edge on
    either axis.
  - Overlap and patches: every pair of flowers is at least 1.05 (R1 + R2) apart on five sizes and densities. The
    patch count is exactly `max(1, round(area/(190000·S²)·density))`, summed per screen. Doubling the density gives

- **Head spring:**
  - With no wind, a nod rings 6 flower heads at their f0 within 1.1 %, and the measured ζ is within 0.002 of 0.12.
  - At breeze 3 with a wild pointer for 60 s, no head ever swings past 0.45 R.

  - 30 clicks on the most open spot give exactly 1 bloom and 29 touches, and every head stays within its cap.
  - A click off the meadow is ignored.
  - With a limit of 5, the open clicked flowers never exceed 5, and they close oldest first. The last one fades in

## Rulings

1. **The pointer's path is played back 0.1 s behind, from timed samples** (`Stir.pathLag`, the re-check's finding
   7).
   - How: `pointerMoved(x:y:at:)` records each sample with its time on the breeze's input clock (`dt / speed` a
     step). Each step, the stir point moves to where the path was 0.1 s ago, interpolated, and the sketch's
     `stirStep` stirs that segment. Samples later than the lag are caught up in one step, so no way is dropped.
   - Why: per-step averaging alone, which I tried first, still differed by 14–22 % for bursts, because only the
     samples' times can tell late input from fast input.
   - With no lag it is the sketch's rule exactly. The replays run it that way and match.
   - Cost if wrong: the pointer's air trails it by 0.1 s, and `setPathLag(0)` gives the sketch's rule back.
   - The host has to pass event times: unstamped bursts differ by 25.5 % (see Concerns).
2. **"Gap" and the 10 pt slack, per screen.**
   - A gap is a point inside the terrain's bounds that no screen holds.
   - A patch's blooms stand on its own screen, or up to 10 pt past one of its real edges on either axis, as the
     sketch's per-axis test does. They never stand past a shared edge onto the next screen, and never in a gap.
   - Each screen gets `max(1, round(…))` patches of its own; patch spacing counts every screen's centres.
   - With one screen this is exactly the sketch (the replays confirm it).
3. **Relayout fades more than the brief names.** Any clicked flower off the terrain fades, at any size of change,
   as the brief asks. I also fade a planted flower whose centre can no longer stand (off the screens, or now in a
   gap). In a big replant, a clicked flower off the screens fades instead of vanishing as in the sketch.
   - Cost if wrong: a planted flower near a moved edge fades rather than lingering off screen.
4. **The pollen sparks are drawn inside `Flowers`,** into a fixed 256-spark buffer that the world takes with
   `takeSparks`.
   - Why: a click's spark draws come between the flower's draws and the answer draw on the input stream, so drawing
     them later would shift the stream.
   - Consequence: the world (m6 and m7) must draw the click answer (`rnd() < 0.7`) after `click` returns. The puff's
     motion is m4's.
5. **A click off the terrain is ignored.** The sketch blooms wherever the canvas gets a click, but the port's
   clicks are only ever inside the screens (m7's rule). This is why the probes' click points are fractions of each
   viewport.
6. **A flower's reach uses `makeFlower`'s formula** (the longest leaf at full length). The sketch's `leaves` slider
   handler recomputes reach with a leaves factor; since the leaves value is fixed in the app, that only matters to
   the painter (m4).
7. **The wind-peak check runs an hour** (172 800 samples: 24 points every 0.5 s) and holds the peak to 88–100 pt/s. Over 10 minutes the
   peak is about 83 (see Concerns).
8. **Two probe fixtures of my own** were made with a probe script appended to a scratch copy of the sketch (never
   the sketch itself) before the real fixture landed. They are kept because they cover what the fixture doesn't: a
   second cell size, every flower field, and the pollen count. I re-recorded them once:
   - first, to undo the sketch's opening moment, which nods and books flowers for its butterflies before the probe
     empties them;
   - second, to keep the click points on each viewport.

## Concerns

- **The fixture will be re-exported** by s1 fix round 3 (the clock fields only; the samples are to stay identical).
  My copy is 4bcf693's. The replay never reads `step.dt` and takes the last tick from the samples, so the new file
  should drop in unchanged. It still has to be copied over when round 3 lands, and the check run again.
- **The wind "peaks at about 90–100":**
  - The formula's top is 26 · 1.7 · 1.1 · 1.9 = 92.4 pt/s at S = 1.
  - The sketch's base-speed noise rarely nears its extreme, so over 10 minutes the port, like the sketch, peaks near
    83 pt/s; over an hour, 90.9. The mean is 21 pt/s.
  - The air is the sketch's exactly (see the replays), so the "about 95" in the inventory reads high.
- **The host must pass event times** (`pointerMoved(at:)`) on the breeze's input clock, from the events'
  timestamps, for bursty input to stir the same air. This is for m7. Without times, bursts of four differ by 25 %,
  which is still better than the sketch's rule.
- **The allocation check relies on the allocator's logging hook** (`malloc_logger`, found with `dlsym`). Where it
  can't be watched, the check prints a skip and passes rather than failing. On this Mac it counted (0 allocations;
  a deliberate append counted 8 in a trial).
