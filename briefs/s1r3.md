# Brief s1, round 3: the Meadow sketch moves and lives like the family

Size: Design (look development; approved; no review)

Branch: meadow-sketch (continue on it, from d893c50)

## Objective
The director played with round 2 and sent their settings "for now" (on the board, Corrections, 2026-11-20). The look
is accepted for now. This round is about MOTION and BEHAVIOUR: the details that make the sharks and spiders feel
alive, brought to the butterflies so the Meadow feels like the same family in motion too.

How to read the director's settings: count 7 (default 4), speed 2 (1), lurch 2 (the slider's top), zig-zag 1.29,
glide 0.44 (1), breeze 1.7, turning 1.23. They want **more travel and more vigour**. Their earlier notes say the
same: "not enough movevemt", "they gather around to mcuh", "the wing beat loosk too infreent and slow", "it doenst
move like a butterfly anomore". So nothing you add may make the butterflies sit more or move less by default. Stillness
is there to give the motion meaning, and stays short.

## Read first
`docs/plans/2026-11-20-family-details.md` (on main): §2.2–2.6 (the family's motion, behaviour, interaction, traces,
edges, with numbers and file references) and the gap tables §3.2–3.6 with suggestions. They are your spec. Check the
family code they cite when a number matters. §3.7 (performance) only as far as needed to stay smooth.

## Do, in this order
1. **The director's settings become the defaults** (every reset goes back to them). Raise the lurch slider's top
   to 3, since the director hit 2. Keep the `count` slider as the director set it; don't replace it with density
   by area (put an "area-scaled" switch next to it, off by default, if you want to show it).
2. **Nothing snaps:** take-off turns through the heading spring and blends the stroke from the landed pose into
   the beat; landing eases the stroke into the pose and brakes over the last beats (§3.2).
3. **Enter and leave through edges:** raising the count brings butterflies in from a screen edge; lowering it sends
   them out through the nearest edge; at load, the opening moment from §3.3 (one nectaring, one basking, one about
   to land, the rest flying).
4. **Hold still and one lands on the cursor** (§3.4): pointer still ≥ 3 s, it spirals in, sits, opens and closes,
   and any move sends it off in an escape burst. A switch for it.
5. **Senses, not knows, and the air carries the pointer** (§3.3, §3.4): a sensing radius for flowers with a forward
   bias; landed butterflies feel the stirred air (wings close, then flush); a small alarm relay to near neighbours,
   never a whole-screen scatter; touch-and-go on some approaches.
6. **Wants and temperament** (§3.3): hunger and warmth drive visits and basking, tuned so that at the director's
   settings the share of time in the air is no lower than now; a boldness per butterfly; spacing only among
   similar heights.
7. **Small life when landed** (§3.2): breathing, an antenna twitch, a small heading shuffle, a lean and part-close
   in a gust.
8. **Traces** (§3.5): wing downwash on the grass under low butterflies; the flower nods when one lands and a little
   when it leaves. Spent flowers are optional.
9. Every new behaviour gets a slider or switch with its own ↺ reset and a short hint, grouped so the panel still
   reads.
