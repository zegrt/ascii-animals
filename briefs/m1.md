# Brief m1: the Meadow's air and flowers, from above

Size: Content
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: meadow-m1, made from main (the first task of the port's fresh stack)

## Objective
Port the sketch's air and flowers to Swift, faithfully. The original is `Tools/meadow-sketch/meadow.html` on
`meadow-sketch` at the commit the board names as re-checked (s1 row). The director tuned it by eye; the app must
behave like it at the director's settings. Plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-20-meadow-port-plan.md` (task m1, "How we keep
it faithful", "Carried from the sketch review"). Map: `docs/plans/2026-11-20-meadow-port-inventory.md` (§1 "Breeze and
pointer air", "Flowers and planting", "Clicks"; §2 rows Breeze and Flowers; §3; §4 items 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 11).

The side-view code on `meadow-t1-2` (Breeze.swift, Flowers.swift) is a reference only: take its finite guards and
typed-expression style, not its model (it assumes a ground band and wind along x only).

## Do
1. **`Meadow/MeadowTuning.swift`:** every sketch default and constant this task uses, named as in the sketch
   (slider keys and constant names), with the sketch line it comes from in a comment. Later tasks add theirs here.
2. **`Meadow/MeadowMath.swift`:** the sketch's seeded generator (Mulberry32), its `hash3`, value noise and `smooth`,
   ported exactly so a seed gives the same numbers as the sketch; two streams as the sketch now has (the meadow's
   own and input's); a seeded Fisher–Yates shuffle. Reuse `WebMath` only where it is the same function.
3. **`Meadow/Breeze.swift`:** the sketch's wind (slowly turning direction, base speed, gust bands rolling downwind),
   the pointer stir grid (48·S pt cells, radius 3, decay 1.6/s, clamp ±260) fed once per step from the pointer's
   movement since the last step (the sketch's `stirStep`, never per input event), the downwash field, and the three
   air sums (all air; what a landed butterfly feels; the pointer's stir alone). S = cell width / 13.
4. **`Meadow/Flowers.swift`:** the seven kinds and their numbers, the leaf rosettes and mustard florets as data,
   planting by patches (area-based count, best-of-10 spacing, no overlap at 1.05), per screen rect of a `Terrain`
   (no flower centre in a gap; patches may straddle a real edge, never a shared one), the head spring in the wind
   (ζ 0.12, cap 0.45 R), growth with its overshoot, the feeding lift and the visited dim, flower ids (never array
   indices), and clicks: touch a bloom (nod, pollen count, release its visitor through a callback), bloom only with
   room, the click limit with the oldest fading over 2 s, and the replant transition the sketch's fix round made.
5. Fixed 1/60 s steps only (the family's `OceanClock` drives them); no frame-time stepping. No allocation per
   step once warmed.
6. **The replay fixture:** the sketch's fix round 2 (running in parallel, `briefs/s1f2.md`) commits
   `Tools/meadow-sketch/fixtures/air-flowers.json` on `meadow-sketch` (seed, normalized settings, viewport, cell size,
   a pointer trace, sampled air and flower state at fixed ticks). Copy it into the checks' resources and replay it in
   Swift, comparing within stated tolerances. If it hasn't landed when you reach this step, do the rest first and
   ask the manager.
7. **Carried from the sketch's re-check** (board, Review routing, s1): clicked flowers left off screen by any resize
   fade out (no 80 px threshold); the same pointer path gives the same air whether input arrives at 15, 30, 60 or
   120 Hz, regularly or in bursts (integrate the pointer's path per step, never per event, never dropping distance
   when two steps' worth arrives together); the tuning file states each rule as the sketch actually runs it.

## Checks (in `OceanChecks/MeadowChecks.swift`, registered in `OceanChecks/main.swift`)
- Same seed, same meadow and air; the replay fixture matches.
- Stir half-life ≈ 0.43 s and stays local; the same pointer path at 15, 30, 60 and 120 Hz input gives the same air
  within 5 %.
- Downwash only under z < 0.35; wind at breeze 1.7 peaks within the sketch's range (about 90–100 pt/s).
- No flower centre in the gap of a two-rect terrain; no overlap below 1.05; patch count follows area and density.
- The head spring rings near f0 with ζ 0.12 and stays within 0.45 R.
- Thirty clicks on one spot touch, never stack; clicked flowers never exceed the limit; the oldest fades over 2 s.
- Finite on bad input (NaN, infinity, zero or huge viewports).
Never edit or loosen a check to pass.
