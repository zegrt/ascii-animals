## Fix (the review's numbers)
1. Count settles at the edges: exit completes on the rendered footprint; entry completes on an inset that fits
   the viewport, also on tiny viewports.
2. Pause: no unbounded growth while paused (clicks while paused are bounded or ignored; say which and why).
3. Validate and normalize stored and URL settings against the control schema before building anything.
4. One replant transition for Reset all, New meadow, resize and Density / Flower size: it releases affected
   butterflies through take-off, applies the click limit, keeps clicked flowers on screen, supersedes pending
   replants.
5. The cursor approach ends on pointer leave, on "Land on a still pointer" Off, and on resize to an invalid point;
   the body stays inside the meadow at edges.
6. Re-enabling species brings them back: the mix follows the enabled species at a full count (through leaving and
   entering, no pop).
7. Pointer wind is integrated in simulation time, so the same gesture gives the same air at 30, 60 or 120 events/s.
9. One size rule for the panel, the clipboard and the drawing (if Size down may exceed Size up, say what that
   means and make all three agree); clicked flowers follow Flower size.
10. Only the primary button's own press-and-release clicks; cancel the gesture on leave, blur or capture loss.
11. Make each hint say the rule as it is (spacing at different heights, air close and flush blending, the opening
    moment at zero visits, the Stay floor). Keep the behaviour unless a hint can't be made honest.
12. The click-answer gap, the hold threshold wording and the nearest blocking bloom, as the review says.
13. Move the stored settings key on, so every browser loads the true defaults once.
14. Space on a focused control activates it; shortcuts only outside controls.
15. (sketch part) Seeded shuffles; UI and render randomness on their own stream, so a seed replays the same flight.

8 and the rest of 15 go to the port plan (not here), except 8's paused-backlog part, which 2 fixes.
