# Brief d1: the Meadow in the app, designed for the director (presets, Settings, menu, what's-new)

Size: Design (a written proposal with pictures; nothing built; the director approves it before m8 and t9)
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: design-d1, made from main

## Objective
While the port (mp) is being built, design how people meet and choose the Meadow in the app, so m8 and t9 can be
built straight from an approved design. The director's answers so far (WORKBOARD.md, Corrections and Decisions):
- settings are "presets like the other two animas": named butterfly line-ups like Ocean's shark presets and Web's
  spider presets, with Custom (`Sources/ASCIIAnimals/Presets.swift`, `SettingsView.swift`);
- "whats new doesnt need a full screen thing": the 3.1 what's-new for existing users is not the full-screen
  welcome window the Web card uses today (`OnboardingController.swift`); the manager suggested a small card under
  the menu bar icon, shown once, with "Try the Meadow" and "Not now", and a "New" tag on Meadow in the Habitat
  menu; the first-run welcome for new users stays as it is;
- no monarch life cycle or courtship in 3.1; "land on a still pointer" follows the app's interaction setting.
The original plan's Task 9 (docs/plans/2026-11-19-meadow-3.1-plan.md) has earlier wording to start from.

## Do
1. Look at how Ocean and Web appear today: take the app's own snapshots (read `--snapshot-*` flags in
   `Sources/ASCIIAnimals`, run only with `ASCII_ANIMALS_NO_HANDOVER=1` and a snapshot flag) of Settings (Ocean and
   Web sections, dark and light), the welcome's first beat and the Web card. Look at them.
2. Propose, in the family's voice and layout:
   - **Meadow presets:** 4–6 named butterfly line-ups (species and how many on a laptop screen, scaled by screen
     size), the default being the director's tuned mix (all four species, 7 on a laptop), plus Custom.
   - **The Settings section** for the Meadow, matching the Ocean and Web sections.
   - **The menu:** Habitat gains Meadow (with a "New" tag for 3.1); what the Meadow's own submenu holds.
   - **The status word** ("Fluttering", as "Swimming" and "Weaving").
   - **What's-new for existing users:** small, not full screen; show two or three options as pictures and
     recommend one.
   - **The welcome for new users:** what changes (the habitat chips; the Meadow's lines).
3. Draw each as a mock-up picture in the app's real look (an HTML page rendered to PNG headless, matching the
   snapshots' fonts and colours), dark and light.
