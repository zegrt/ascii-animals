# Brief m8: the Meadow offered in the app, with its presets and settings

Size: Content
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: meadow-m8, made from `meadow-mp` (mp is in review; mp's fix rounds stay out of this task's Files)

## Objective
Offer the Meadow in the app, built from the approved design: `docs/plans/2026-11-20-meadow-in-the-app.md` on
`design-d1` (f813cc9), with the director's answers (WORKBOARD.md, Decisions, 2026-11-20):
- **Three presets plus Custom:** "Mixed" (default: the director's mix, all four species, 7 on a laptop),
  "Just a Few", "Blue Morphos". Custom appears by itself when nothing matches.
- **Settings:** a Meadow section like Web's, with the line-up picker, four species switches (at least one stays on),
  "How many" (1–12, the laptop number, scaled by screen area on bigger displays), and Speed.
- **"Land on a still pointer"** and clicks follow the app's existing interaction setting.
- **The menu:** Habitat gains Meadow with a "New" tag that goes once it's picked; a Butterflies submenu with the
  line-ups and "Edit Line-Up…". The status word is "Fluttering".

The what's-new card and the welcome are t9, not this task.

## Do
1. `HabitatKind.offered` gains `.meadow` (mp added the list).
2. The Meadow's settings in `Settings` (tolerant decoding and `sanitized()` as the family does), feeding `MeadowSim`:
   the species on, how many per laptop-sized screen (scaled by area per screen as the design says), the speed.
3. `Presets.swift`, `SettingsView.swift`, `StatusMenu.swift`, `OceanStatus.swift` as the design shows, dark and light.
4. The Settings preview (340 × 150) and the About banner show the Meadow well (mp checked the tiny viewport).
5. Snapshots with the app's own `--snapshot-*` flags of Settings on the Meadow (dark, light) and the menu; compare
   with the design's pictures and look at them.
