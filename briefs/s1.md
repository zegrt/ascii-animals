# Brief s1: the Meadow sketch, a live web original with sliders

Size: Design, approved by the director ("ok", 2026-11-20, to: "a quick web page of the Meadow that you open in your
browser, with sliders ... once you're happy, we build that into the app"). It is look development: not reviewed
now; the Swift port that follows is built and reviewed in the rewritten plan.
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: meadow-sketch, made from main

## Objective
The sharks and spiders came from finished web originals that were then ported to Swift. The Meadow has none, and
every look decision so far was made before the director could see it. Make that original: one self-contained HTML
page with the top-down Meadow running live, and sliders the director drags to set the look and the realism line
themselves. When they say "that's it", its numbers are frozen and ported to the app.

## Read first
- `WORKBOARD.md`, Corrections section: every note the director has given on the Meadow, newest first. They are the
  acceptance test. In short: seen from above with depth (butterflies come closer and go further); few; spread over
  the whole screen; always moving; move like butterflies (jerky, tied to the wingbeat); wingbeat faster than now;
  both wings beat together; butterflies turn to face where they fly; wings shaded and detailed, not flat colour,
  stylised not photo-real; mainly black with hints of green; much better flowers.
- `docs/plans/2026-11-20-meadow-top-down-direction.md` (the picture).
- `git show look-top-down:reports/p2a.md`: why the p1 prototype stopped moving like a butterfly, and how the
  measured flight maps to the view from above (lurch and jinks in the plane, lift and gravity on height, the bob as a
  size and shadow pulse).
- The measured flight and species numbers: `Sources/OceanKit/Meadow/Butterfly.swift`, `ButterflySpecies.swift`
  (on `meadow-t5v`); `docs/plans/2026-11-19-meadow-research.md` §1, §4, §5.4.
- Wing shapes, markings and palettes: `Sources/OceanKit/Meadow/MeadowPainter.swift` (on `meadow-t5v`); flowers and
  ground seen from above: `Sources/OceanTool/ProtoPainter.swift` and `ProtoMeadow.swift` (on `proto-top-down`).
- The wingbeat reference (learn from it, don't copy): https://www.pocketstudio.design/ascii-animals-app/
- How the app draws: a character grid of 13 × 19 pt cells, Geist Mono SemiBold (on Google Fonts), each cell a
  block colour plus a glyph; dark and light themes; Calm dims. Match that so the port is faithful.

## What to build
`Tools/meadow-sketch/meadow.html`, one file, plain JS and canvas, no libraries (Geist Mono from Google Fonts only).
- The Meadow fills the page and runs at 60 Hz steps: about 4 butterflies of the four species, seen from above with
  height (size and shadow), turning to face where they fly, the measured flight turned on its side, both wings
  beating together with shading and the species markings; flowers seen from above in planted patches; ground
  mainly black with hints of green; grass that ripples where the breeze passes; the pointer stirs the breeze and a
  click blooms a flower. A simple want is enough (fly, visit a flower, bask, move on); no life cycle.
- A slider panel the director can open and hide, grouped and labelled in plain words, each with a one-line hint,
  and a reset. At least: wingbeat speed (× real), zig-zag, gliding, travel speed, number of butterflies, size
  range (low/high), time spent high vs low, how long they stay on a flower, flower density, flower size, breeze
  strength, shadow strength, ground green amount, slow motion (time scale), species on/off, dark/light, Calm.
  Show the current values so the director can say them back.
- A "copy settings" button that copies all slider values as JSON (for the port).
- It must run smoothly in a browser at full window size.

## Publishing
Follow the Artifact page contract: a `<title>` "Meadow Sketch" at the top with
`<meta name="claude:viewport-fit-sides" content="cover">`; colours as tokens; body background set; works at phone
width (the panel collapses); no external scripts; fonts from Google Fonts only. The manager publishes it: when it
looks right to you, say so in the report. Also save one or two PNG screenshots (dark and light) if you can render
them (for example with a headless browser), else describe what you see.

## Output and done when
`Tools/meadow-sketch/meadow.html` committed on `meadow-sketch` with `reports/s1.md` (what each slider does, the
defaults you chose and why, what reads well and what doesn't). No local folder paths in commits or files.

## Out of scope
The Swift app, checks, settings, the life cycle, courtship. Nothing in `Sources/` changes.
