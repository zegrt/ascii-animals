## Objective
The director, after playing with the sketch: "figure all all the small devil in the edtails type stuff from those
scnees to this one can sfeel like the same family", clarified as the visual details; "aslo need reset button
seperately for each tweak"; "aslo colors in light mode doesnt look right"; and earlier, the coloured glyph noise the
sharks' and spiders' bodies have. This round is about the LOOK. Motion and behaviour gaps stay for later.

## Read first
`docs/plans/2026-11-20-family-details.md` (on main): the full catalogue of Ocean's and Web's look with numbers and
file references, and the gap list against this sketch. Section 2.1 and gap table 3.1 are your spec; 3.5's visual
traces are second priority. Check the family code it cites when a number matters.

## Do, in this order
1. **The mosaic:** blocks inset one point from the top-left grid lines, as Ocean and Web draw them; grid lines at
   row and column 0 too.
2. **Edges like the family:** wing (and petal) cells with coverage under 0.6 drop the block and show only a light
   glyph (`: ;` from 0.5, else `. ' , \``), at the family's level formula.
3. **Carved dark glyphs on lit blocks:** wing field glyphs from the skin ramp `: ; t % S X 8 # @` by shade with a ±1
   random step, in dark carved ink as the family does, with few empty cells (the white may keep more). Keep the
   black marks' glyphs.
4. **The hide shimmer** (Web's rule): a per-cell re-roll about 2.4 times a second on staggered phases; on a roll ~6 %
   of cells take one of the family's seven accent colours (by key), ~4 % a white fleck (dark for the cabbage
   white), edge cells 7 %; not on marks, eyespots or veins, so species still read; flowers at half the rate. A
   "Sparkle" slider. Calm × 0.35. Off on paper, as Web.
5. **Depth in brightness:** about 0.78 + 0.28 × height, so near and far read in light as well as size.
6. **Small anatomy:** two eye cells beside the head on bigger butterflies, in the family's eye ink; a few leg cells
   when landed with wings closed or in a vee.
7. **Thin lines like silk:** veins, petal and fennel rays, and grass use Web's line glyphs, including `¯ _ -` by
   where a level line sits in its cell.
8. **Light mode, redone the family way:** make it match how Web (and Ocean) look on paper, not a separate scheme.
   Render Web's and Ocean's light stills with `OceanTool` for comparison (look at `OceanTool`'s usage), and judge
   side by side.
9. **A reset beside every slider and choice,** back to its default; "Reset" stays as reset-all.
10. Second priority, if time allows: gold motes where the pointer stirs the air; a flower dims its disc for 20–40 s
    after a visit.
