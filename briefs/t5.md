  viewport: CGRect, grid: GridSpec, theme: ThemeParams, into canvas: CellCanvas)`. t3 will wrap it for its world.
- The life-story drawing (caterpillar, chrysalis, the empty case) is NOT in this task; it moves to t4.
- The GPU check `meadowComposeDraws` goes through `encode(habitat:)` using a small test-only `CellPainter` wrapper
  inside OceanChecks (land + butterflies + breeze + this painter), as 3.0 did before its habitat existed.
- Add `OceanTool meadow-still <out.png> <W>x<H> [--light] [--calm] [--seconds S] [--seed N]`: lay out the land on one
  rect, fly a few of each species with `Butterfly.fly` toward random flowers for S seconds (default 20), then paint
  one frame and write the PNG. Set each butterfly's `scale` from the cell (`cell.width / 13`).

Why restart: the look now passes (all seven poses of all four species read). What keeps failing is that the flight
and the painter each have their own idea of where the butterfly is and how big it is: the flight's point drifts
from the drawing (up to 86 pt at a low flower, 85 pt on the preview), the wall stop sees a point, not a shape, so
wings poke into the gap between offset displays, and the banked clap still flips colour in one step. Patching each
case has not converged.

### The design change: one butterfly geometry, shared
- A single type in `OceanKit/Meadow` (for example `ButterflyShape`) gives, for a butterfly's current pose and size:
  its body centre, its mouth (contact) point, and its drawn envelope in whole cells (wings, tails, antennae, with
  tilt). The painter draws from it, and the flight bounds itself with it. Nothing else computes extents.
- `(x, y)` stays the body centre everywhere. At a flower, the mouth point goes to the flower head and the body sits
  where the shape says; the proboscis is drawn at the mouth point.
- The flight keeps the whole envelope inside the terrain, a union of screen rects, corners and gaps included: no
  cell of the envelope may lie outside every rect.
- A screen too small for a full-size butterfly (the 340 × 150 preview) gets a smaller size for its butterflies,
  chosen at layout, so the drawing is always where the butterfly is. No offset drawing.
- The colour change from top side to underside is continuous at every bank, not only level flight.
