# Brief m2: flight from above

Size: Content
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: meadow-m2, made from `meadow-m1` (the port's stack; m1 must be reviewed first)

## Objective
Port the sketch's butterfly flight to Swift, faithfully: four species flying over the meadow seen from above, with
height z (0 on a flower, 1 just under the glass), the wingbeat, the lurch on each downstroke, zig-zag jinks and
slips, short glides, the bob, turning to face where they fly, and the edges. The original is
`Tools/meadow-sketch/meadow.html` on `meadow-sketch` at 4e1b869 (the board's s1 row). The director tuned it by eye
("it doenst move like a butterfly anomore" was the note that led here); the app must move like the sketch at the
director's settings. Plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-20-meadow-port-plan.md` (task m2, "How we keep it faithful"). Map:
`docs/plans/2026-11-20-meadow-port-inventory.md` (§1 "Butterfly flight from above", "Wingbeat and turning"; §2 rows
Species and Flight; §4 items 1, 2, 5).

The side-view `meadow-t1-2` flight is a reference only (finite guards, typed expressions, the clap structure); it
assumes gravity on screen y and is replaced.

## Do
1. **Species:** the sketch's species table (span, aspect, stroke top and bottom, real beat, cruise and forage pace,
   surge, thrust, bout, glide seconds, jink chance and spread, slip, heading spring and damping, bob, max bank,
   bound, flower weights, nectar and bask ranges, poses), into `MeadowTuning` and `ButterflySpecies`. Drop columns
   the sketch never uses (say which).
2. **Flight** (`Butterfly`): the sketch's model exactly: speed `cruise·S·speed·pace` with the approach slowdown; the
   heading spring with its clamp and the jink decay; the lurch spread over the downstroke and the drag toward the
   floor; the clap (jinks, slips, the white's surge, the morpho's bounds on z); height toward `zBase` with its
   noise, boldness offset and spring; glides that start at the glide pose after a bout and sink; the bob; ground
   velocity = airspeed + 0.55 × wind (m1's `Breeze`); bank and the beat-coupled yaw; the soft take-off and landing
   blends as state the painter and world will read. Targets (waypoints, flowers, the pointer) come in from outside;
   m6 decides them. Fixed 1/60 s steps; no allocation per step.
3. **Edges:** steer back once the body is 0.25 span past a real screen edge; shared edges between screens are open;
   the body is never placed in a gap (use `Terrain`). Leaving through an edge as a mode the world can ask for.
4. **The wingbeat cap:** keep the sketch's 7.5 Hz cap. Do not cap by frame rate: the motion must not depend on fps
   (the director decides the 15 fps question from m6's film).
5. **Replay:** the sketch's `default.json` fixture (format 2; replay at exactly 1/60 s per tick) holds butterfly
   samples; replay what m2 can (flight with targets fed as the sketch fed them, or a flight-only scenario you add to
   the comparison) and state the tolerances. If the fixture can't isolate flight, say so and add a tighter
   comparison of your own against the sketch run headless.
6. **A track picture** (`OceanTool` subcommand): each species' tracks over a minute, from above, next to the sketch's
   at the same seed; look at it.

## Checks (`OceanChecks/MeadowFlightChecks.swift`, registered in `OceanChecks/main.swift`)
- Beat = real × wingbeat (0.5) within the sketch's ±10 %, never over 7.5 Hz.
- The lurch: a ground-speed pulse once per beat on the downstroke.
- The monarch's heading varies less than the white's.
- Glide share at glide 0.1 near the sketch's (state the measured sketch figure).
- z always in [0, 1], with the bob amplitude.
- Coverage: each species over 3 minutes visits most cells of a coarse grid over the screen (no clustering, the
  director's first note).
- The body never more than 0.25 span past a real edge and never in a gap of a two-rect terrain.
- Deterministic for a seed; finite on bad input.
Never edit or loosen a check to pass.
