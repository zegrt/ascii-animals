# Brief p1: a throwaway prototype of the Meadow seen from above, with depth

Size: Prototype (throwaway: never merged, no review; it exists only so the director can choose the view by eye)
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: proto-top-down, made from `meadow-t5v`

## Objective
The director is choosing between the current side view and a meadow seen from above, where butterflies can come
closer to and go further from the viewer. Read `docs/plans/2026-11-20-meadow-top-down-direction.md` first: it is
the picture to prototype, and it lists the director's feedback it answers. Make a 30 s film the director can watch
next to the side-view film (t5v's) and pick from.

## What to build (quick, not production)
An `OceanTool meadow-proto <dir> <W>x<H> [--seconds S] [--light] [--seed N]` command that writes frames
(`f%05d.png`), built in new files as far as possible. Reuse what exists (Breeze, Butterfly flight for heading, beat,
jinks and glides, the painter's wing drawing and species), and fake what doesn't exist yet. Aim for:
- **Seen from above, with height:** each butterfly has a height z (0 on a flower, 1 near the viewer). Drawn size
  grows with height (roughly 0.6 × to 1.6 ×); a faint shadow on the meadow, offset by height, meets it on landing;
  nearer ones draw over farther ones. Flapping bouts climb, glides sink (the flight's vertical physics moved to z).
- **Turning:** each butterfly drawn rotated to face where it flies, turning smoothly.
- **Wingbeat:** both wings together, never vanishing, a first designed rhythm (learn from the director's reference,
  https://www.pocketstudio.design/ascii-animals-app/, without copying it), with some shading on the wings.
- **Flowers from above:** blooms (petal rings round a centre, umbels, clover) in loose patches across the whole
  screen, sparse and dim; a faint grass texture that brightens where the breeze passes.
- **About 4 butterflies** of all four species, roaming the whole screen, sinking to a flower now and then for a few
  seconds (wings open flat when basking), then rising and moving on; free to fly off an edge and come back.

## Output
Films at 1440 × 900 (H.264 via /opt/homebrew/bin/ffmpeg): 30 s dark at real speed, 10 s at quarter speed, 15 s
light; and one still. Watch them and describe in `reports/p1.md` what reads well and what doesn't, honestly, and
what a real build would need. Film paths only in the final message.

## Files
New files under `Sources/OceanTool/` (and, if unavoidable, new files under `Sources/OceanKit/Meadow/` named
`Proto*`), plus the command line in `Sources/OceanTool/main.swift`. Don't change existing behaviour; the whole suite
must still pass.

## Out of scope
Production quality, checks for the prototype, settings, the app. Nothing here is merged.
