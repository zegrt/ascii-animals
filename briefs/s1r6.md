# Brief s1, round 6: light mode, designed for paper

Size: Design (look development; approved; no review)
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: meadow-sketch (continue on it, from b01db3f)

## Objective
The director: "aslo colors in light mode doesnt look right" (before round 2), and now: "also to be clear is the
colors in light mode not beign correct intended?". Round 2 tried, but it converted the dark palette to paper by a
formula. It still looks wrong (board, Corrections, top entry):
- the black markings are solid near-black blocks: heavy and muddy on paper;
- wing colours turn muddy (monarch brown, swallowtail olive, morpho dull);
- white flowers turn grey and vanish into the paper;
- the leaves, pale mint, are the loudest colour on screen.

The family on paper uses pale tinted blocks with dark ink glyphs carved on them, and no solid black: Ocean's shark is
pale blue-grey tiles with dark glyphs; Web's spider is pale tan tiles with dark glyphs.

## Do
1. **Look first.** Render Ocean's and Web's light stills with `OceanTool` (read its usage in
   Sources/OceanTool/main.swift; stills to your own temp folder) and the sketch's light mode, crop them side by
   side, and write down in the report what the family's paper look is made of: block lightness and saturation, glyph
   ink, how dark markings are shown, how the ground reads. Read the family's light-mode code where it helps
   (research doc §2.1 "Theme, Calm, dissolve" cites it).
2. **Design a paper palette** for the Meadow from that, not a formula over the dark one:
   - wings as pale tinted blocks in each species' own hue, clearly a monarch orange, a morpho blue, a swallowtail
     yellow, a white that reads on paper (a faint warm or cool tint plus ink edges);
   - dark markings (monarch veins and border, swallowtail stripes, morpho edges) as dark ink glyphs on pale
     blocks or a mid-tone, never solid black blocks; the species must still read at a glance;
   - flowers that read on paper, white daisies included (ink and a faint tint, not grey);
   - leaves and grass pushed back so nothing on the ground is louder than the butterflies;
   - shadows, the rim (a paper halo), depth (paler further away) and gold motes all checked on paper.
3. Dark mode must not change: a dark-mode frame is byte for byte as round 5.
4. Check Calm on paper, and Depth 0, 1 and 2 on paper.
