# Brief s1, round 5: near and far read apart

Size: Design (look development; approved; no review)
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: meadow-sketch (continue on it, from f8811f6)

## Objective
The director, on round 4: "looks good one last thing rn there is no seperation between the close butterfies and
the stuff far away especially the flowers". They approved this approach ("ok").

Why it happens: on a fixed character grid a near thing can't show coarser texture, the eye's main depth cue; every
tile is the same size. So the other cues must carry depth, and today they barely do: butterflies are drawn at 0.78–1.06
brightness by height, while flowers sit at full brightness and colour, with the same tiles, carving and sparkle. A
low butterfly is darker than the flowers under it.

## Do
1. **Push the meadow back with light, the family's way** (research doc §2.1 "Depth and near/far": Ocean's far fish
   0.3–0.7, Web's old webs 0.5–0.6, dim factors like 0.7 + 0.3 × depth). Flowers, leaves and grass drawn at about
   0.65–0.75 brightness, a little less saturated, with half the sparkle. Butterflies stay full and get brighter as
   they rise, so even a landed butterfly reads in front of its flower. A flower brightens back a little while a
   butterfly feeds on it. Keep the flowers' colours recognisable: they must still look like the better flowers the
   director asked for, just further away.
2. **A dark rim around high butterflies:** a one-cell rim of dark ink round the silhouette (the family's dark ink,
   over whatever is behind), strong near the glass, fading out as the butterfly comes down to land, so it cuts
   out from the meadow. Make it look like part of the family's mosaic, not a cartoon outline.
3. **One Depth slider** (with ↺ and a hint) runs all of it from 0, today's flat look, to a strong separation. Set
   its default where it looks right to you, and say why.
4. **If 1 and 2 at a good setting still don't separate them,** add the stronger step: less detail in the distance
   (the ground drawn with fewer carved glyphs, more plain light glyphs, a calmer shimmer), also on the Depth
   slider. Say whether you needed it.
5. Check light mode (on paper, far things go paler rather than darker) and Calm.
