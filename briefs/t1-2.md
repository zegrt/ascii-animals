## Done when
- Every check named in plan Tasks 1 and 2 passes, and so does the whole suite (`swift run -c release OceanChecks`).
- The director's correction of 2026-11-19 is met: free flight is not clustered in the middle of the screen. It
  roams the whole field: soft walls within 1–2 wingspans, turns as a correlated random walk, a slowly drifting
  preferred direction, altitude that wanders and favours the bottom 40 % (where the flowers are), and waypoints
  anywhere in the terrain. A check `freeFlightCoversTheField` holds it: each species flown 3 minutes visits at least
  6 of the 9 cells of a 3 × 3 grid over the field, including the bottom row.
- Code follows the board's Project rules, including the CI Swift 6.3.3 rule.
- The paths picture has been looked at and described in the report.
