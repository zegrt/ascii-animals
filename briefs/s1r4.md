# Brief s1, round 4: clicking, and less gliding

Size: Design (look development; approved; no review)
Board commit: the commit that adds this brief (main)
Branch: meadow-sketch (continue on it, from caca692)

## Objective
The director, after playing with round 3: "umm what happens when i keep clicking at the same point?", and new
settings: the same as the defaults except **glide 0.1** (from 0.44).

What happens today (`bloomAt`): every click pushes another flower, of a random kind, on exactly that spot. They stack
with no limit and "New meadow" keeps them. Each click also calls the nearest travelling butterfly with chance 0.7,
and each stacked flower takes its own visitor, so clicking one spot piles butterflies onto one point: the gathering
the director has asked to avoid ("they gather around to mcuh"). It also grows the per-frame cost without bound.

## Do
1. **Glide 0.1 is the default.** Check that at 0.1 the flight still has its sail-and-sink moments now and then
   (watch it); if 0.1 kills them entirely, say so in the report, don't change the director's number.
2. **A click on or near a bloom touches that bloom; it never stacks a new one.** On a bloom (within about its
   radius): the head gets a nod on its spring along the click, a small puff of pollen sparks, and a butterfly
   sitting on it is flushed (it takes off away from the click, through the existing take-off). Clicking again
   keeps doing that, never piling anything up.
3. **New blooms need room:** a click elsewhere blooms a flower only if it would not overlap another bloom; where
   there's no room, treat it as a touch of the nearest bloom.
4. **Clicked flowers have a limit,** about 12 by default. Beyond it, the oldest clicked flower fades away over a
   couple of seconds (eased, no pop), releasing any visitor through take-off.
5. **One answer at a time:** a fresh bloom calls the nearest travelling butterfly as now, but no more than one
   butterfly answers clicks every few seconds, and never one already heading to a clicked flower.
6. Any number you introduce that the director may want to tune (the limit, the gap between answers) gets a slider
   with its ↺ reset and a hint; update the panel's top line so it says what a click does.
