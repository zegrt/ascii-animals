2. **A replay fixture export** for the Swift port (the review's "What is missing"): `?debug=1&fixture=<name>` runs a
   fixed scenario on the sketch's own 1/60 s step and offers a JSON download (or prints it to the console for a

   a stir pass, a 3.5 s hold, clicks on open ground and on a bloom), and samples at fixed ticks (0, 1, 60, 600,
   1800): the wind at a small grid of points (all air, landed air, pointer stir), every flower's id, kind, centre,
   radius, head offset and age, and every butterfly's id, species, state, position, height, heading and wing
   stroke. Provide two named scenarios: `air-flowers` (no butterflies: count 0) and `default` (the director's
   defaults). Document the format at the top of the export code. Nothing about the sketch's behaviour changes.
