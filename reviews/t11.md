# t11 fix round 2

Verdict: changes requested

Reviewed `971a216..f9bfe12` against the trimmed brief and the rulings in `reports/t11.md`.

## Findings

1. **Should fix — A welcome interrupted by sleep or a screensaver can be marked seen.** The tour or Sharks Web welcome can disappear during its first three seconds and never return on the next launch. `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/OnboardingController.swift:472` acknowledges it after a wall-clock delay if the app is active and the window is key. The power callback at `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/OnboardingController.swift:512` only pauses the habitat when the screen becomes unseeable. A display going to sleep or a screensaver starting does not necessarily produce any of the three window or app notifications that end the welcome. Close it unacknowledged on a visibility interruption and recheck screen eligibility before acknowledging it. Add a check that drives the power callback before the acknowledgement deadline for both variants.

2. **Should fix — Quitting before the Meadow card is readable still spends a showing.** A user who quits or restarts as the card begins to fade in waits three eligible hours on relaunch. If that was the third showing, the card is gone for good. `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/WhatsNewCard.swift:103` and `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/WhatsNewCard.swift:105` save the showing as soon as the panel opens. The refund at `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/WhatsNewCard.swift:202` requires a card close callback, which normal app termination does not invoke. Persist the count when the card reaches its content, and persist explicit dismissals immediately. Check a relaunch from saved state after an unread first and third opening.

3. **Should fix — A burst of wake notifications can open a greeting on a screen that is about to relock.** The waiting card or welcome can appear before the screen has stayed clear for the required second. The welcome can take focus at that point. `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/WhatsNewCard.swift:711` and `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/WhatsNewCard.swift:717` let display notifications and power changes call the presenter immediately. `Sources/OceanKit/Domain/WhatsNew.swift:84` treats two clear calls as a full second. The new check itself makes both calls back to back and expects an opening (`Sources/OceanChecks/GreetingChecks.swift:45`). Keep the first clear time or let only the scheduled confirmation complete the wait. Test that two clear events at the same time leave the greeting waiting, and that a lock cancels the pending confirmation.

4. **Should fix — The welcome asks users to try interactions they cannot see.** With Reduce Motion on or Low Power set to hold a still, moving the pointer or clicking can advance the tour and report success with no visible reaction. `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/OnboardingController.swift:442` now holds the habitat, but the pointer and splash steps still make the same promises and complete from the same inputs (`Sources/ASCIIAnimals/OnboardingController.swift:188`, `Sources/ASCIIAnimals/OnboardingController.swift:664`). The report calls this out as a ruling. Keep the preview policy and skip those demonstrations or give them instructions and feedback that work with a held frame. Check that a user can finish the tour in still mode without being asked to produce an invisible effect.
