# Review m1: the Meadow's air and flowers, from above

Brief: briefs/m1.md      Reviewed commit: 59652168a54e103cae065cc8ee322242bd32f0be

## Design

Changes requested. The weather and flower model is faithful at the director's settings on one screen. Timestamped pointer bursts and flower relayout need fixes before the world builds on these APIs.

- I ran the original sketch at `4e1b869` in headless Chrome. At 1728 × 1117 CSS pixels and 2×, its format-2 `air-flowers` export reproduced the committed file byte for byte with no scenario problems. It has five checkpoints and 295 pointer events. The old and current fixtures differ only in the version and clock fields. With the new file substituted in the detached checkout, the unchanged Swift replay passed at exactly 1/60 s. The maximum differences were 1.6 × 10⁻⁶ pt/s in air and 5.0 × 10⁻⁷ pt in head position.
- At seed 4242 and 1600 × 913, the `OceanTool meadow-air` overlay put all 14 Swift centers on the sketch's blooms. The bright bands in the air plot ran across the downwind arrows. Direct comparisons against the sketch at 1, 3 and 8 s matched the sampled weather and head motion within 2.4 × 10⁻¹⁶ in their respective units. A separate headless run invoked the sketch's flower constructor for all seven kinds, including the full leaf and mustard floret data. Ids, kinds, seeds and counts matched, and the largest numeric difference was under 9 × 10⁻¹⁶. With both pointer stir and flight and feeding downwash applied, all three sampled air vectors matched exactly at five points. The Mulberry32, stream split, hash, value-noise, smoothstep and Fisher–Yates implementations follow the sketch's arithmetic and draw order. The existing golden and replay checks passed.
- A separate headless comparison matched feeding lift and visited dim exactly at two checkpoints. Fifteen clicks, a growing and fading interval, and a non-fresh replant at flower size 1.85 also matched exactly at four checkpoints for draw order, ids, centers, radii, ages and fades. The resize findings below concern the port's separate terrain transition.
- For the wind-peak ruling, the sketch itself gave 82.98 pt/s over ten minutes and 90.908 pt/s over an hour on the check's points at breeze 1.7 and S = 1. The limits of 88–100 pt/s are reasonable as a broad check on that hour-long run, with the replay for tighter parity. The formula's upper bound is 92.38 pt/s. Keep the sketch's constants and treat the inventory's “about 95” as an estimate.
- Bundling `Fixtures` in `Package.swift` is the minimal change required for the requested SwiftPM resource. It affects OceanChecks only, and the board already records the scope addition. Keeping the supplemental probes is justified by the second cell size and extra fields. Ignoring clicks off the screens follows the board's in-terrain rule routed to m7. Drawing pollen inside `Flowers` preserves the input stream before invoking the world callback or drawing a click answer. Document the spark buffer limit and drain contract.
- A lookback is a reasonable way to interpolate timestamped pointer input. The chosen 0.1 s adds 100 ms to the sketch's immediate response. The tested delivery contract has holes even with timestamps, and the host currently supplies position at delivery. Keep the latency choice open until the full matrix passes. The planted-flower fade is a port extension beyond the brief's explicit clicked-flower rule. It makes sense for a bloom displaced by a resize and should work for both small and large changes if retained.
- On local Swift 6.4, `swift build` and `swift build -c release --product ASCIIAnimals` passed with Command Line Tools linker search-path warnings. `swift run -c release OceanChecks` passed **197 checks** on both fixture versions. No existing check changed beyond registering m1, and the first m1 commit already used the 88–100 wind range. The allocator probe reported zero warmed allocations, including a separate run that stepped through four flower expiries. Local type-checking emitted no warnings at 100 ms per expression and 150 ms per function. CI on the reviewed commit used Swift 6.3.3, passed 176 checks with GPU checks disabled, and built the universal app in a job of 4m48s. [CI job](https://github.com/zegrt/ascii-animals/actions/runs/44749143348/job/136332174025)

## What is missing?

The checked-in replays are worth keeping. Add assertions for the rules their traces never exercise, especially a short 15 Hz burst on a fast path, re-entry before the prior path drains, planted and clicked flowers displaced by relayout, and the distinction between the air vectors when downwash is present. Keep the 5% limit and run timestamped events through the real host path when m7 connects it.

The current format-2 fixture is ready to copy as-is. Assert its clock and wind sample structure, and run the replay from the exact viewport-derived points. The part of the fixture without butterflies cannot verify visit dim, feeding lift, flutter or visitor release, so pin those in separate deterministic checks.
