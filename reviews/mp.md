# Review mp: the rest of the Meadow port

Brief: briefs/mp.md      Reviewed commit: 3134721

## Design

Meadow uses the fixed-step habitat interface and the shared cell renderer. The normal app menu, Settings picker and welcome chips use `HabitatKind.offered`, which still contains Ocean and Web in the same order. The menu handler uses that same list for its tags. The settings codec and snapshot override also recognize the new raw value. I checked the app integration from source and by building the app.

### Verification

- `swift build` and `swift build -c release --product ASCIIAnimals` succeeded with Swift 6.4. `swift run -c release OceanChecks` passed **215 checks**, including Metal, the m1 fixtures and m1's warm-step allocation check. The baseline passed 181 checks. I used `debce4e` for the baseline. The subsequent `origin/main` update to `5a113b8` changes only the board entry for CI.

- All eight before-and-after PNG pairs matched byte for byte. For each of Ocean and Web I rendered seed 4242 at 800 × 500 in dark and light, at 1800 × 900 in live Calm at 1.25× scale, and at 340 × 150 in live light Calm.

## What is missing?

- Exercise a narrow gap, an offset pair, a count change, an unplug at the old seam and a pointer held between displays. Check the segment, every position update and the immediate post-layout state. The current gap check uses one static layout at the default count. Its second-screen assertion can pass from initial placement. The relayout check allows a quarter-span outside and skips waiting and departing butterflies.
- Measure allocations after clicking and through the fade and cache eviction. Check retained cache counts on **both** screens during alternating paints and bound the number of fading flowers under an input burst with no simulation ticks.
- Add bad-input probes for the Meadow tool commands and the layout cases below. The current world check passes huge values to pointer input and passes small or empty rectangles to layout.

### What is missing after fixes

- **Several-screen transitions are still incompletely covered.** The new checks exercise narrow and offset gaps, approaches and pulls, count changes, unplug and a pointer starting in a gap. The segment oracle samples at 0.05 pt and misses the zero-length intersection in #2. The immediate relayout check skips departures and does not check surviving nectar routes. The pointer check does not move an already-targeted pointer across the boundary. Those three cases still fail.
- **The click and cache probes are covered.** The new checks alternate two paints, exercise click-time and post-click allocations, check both caches and cover the 2,000-click burst. The wider no-allocation requirement still fails on count changes. After a warm-up on the default shared desk at seed 14, I measured 103 allocations in 90 simulation steps after raising the count from 7 to 12. The first allocation was 48 ticks after `syncFlies()`. The base also allocates (133 allocations in 133 steps with the same setup). An entrant already inside a display calls `Terrain.signedOutside`, which creates an `edges.map` array until the entrant clears the inset. Make the minimum edge-distance scan allocation-free and cover the arrival phase in the allocation check. `MeadowFlight.swift:191`, `Terrain.swift:133`
- **The bad terrain regressions are covered.** CLI rejection is verified by the independent runs above. The checked-in bad-number check calls `ToolInput` helpers and does not exercise argument wiring, process exit or filesystem effects in the tool. `MeadowFixChecks.swift:406`

### Flight judgement

- **Keep four spans for the long-run watchdog.** On the default desk I ran 30 minutes per seed at seeds 1–10 on both commits, measuring every tick and also at the check's every-tenth-tick cadence. Seven of ten seeds on each commit exceeded two spans at the check cadence. The maxima were 3.701 spans sampled and 3.800 at full resolution on the base, versus 2.918 and 2.928 after the fixes. The longest continuous interval at or above two spans dropped from 1.10 s to 0.57 s. The base peak was above the monitor and the fix peak was left of the laptop, so the corner example is one instance of a general real-edge turn. The quarter-span distance remains the threshold for starting the sketch's turn. The measured overshoots were brief and recovered, so this result does not call for changing the flight to satisfy the old two-span threshold. `MeadowFlight.swift:166` and `MeadowWorldChecks.swift:105`
- **Keep exact route checking across real gaps.** On the 1440 × 900 pair at seeds 3 and 11, ten-minute crossing counts were 439/393 with touching edges offset 260 pt, 1/0 at a 1 pt gap, 1/2 at 40 pt and 4/3 at 160 pt. The rare transitions go around the gap, consistent with the segment probes and the brief's open shared edges. At 0.4 pt the terrain's 0.5 pt tolerance treats the seam as open for movement even though `wayOpen` checks the literal union. The practical limit is population. Area-weighted seeding left one of the equal displays empty at the start for 4 of 400 seeds on a 1 pt gap with 2 pt offset, and seed 38 still had nobody on that display after ten minutes. If both displays must have butterflies, assign initial butterflies across disconnected screen groups when the count allows. The geometry should still keep gap crossings rare. `Terrain.swift:28`, `MeadowWorld.swift:154` and `MeadowBehaviour.swift:34`
