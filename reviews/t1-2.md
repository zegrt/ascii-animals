## Design

The approach holds. `Butterfly.fly` follows research §1.4 closely: lift `L·sin θ` on the downstroke only, gravity
= L/π, the forward lurch and the jink at the clap, a heading spring (k, ζ), glides held in the glide pose, and
ground velocity = breeze + airspeed. Walls use `Terrain.wallPush`. The bob falls out of the forces: the lift is
`2·bob·(2πf)²`, which matches the research's "a_osc ≈ 1,300 pt/s² for ±5 pt at 2.6 Hz".

`ButterflySpecies` matches the Recommended parameters table row by row. I checked beat, stroke, cruise/forage,
escape, bouts and glides, glide angle and pose, jink chance and σ, k/ζ, bob, pitch, bank, persistence, dwell, bask,
flower weights, cursor weight, flush, host and pupa. The owner decisions are in: slow motion ≤ 3 Hz, and morphos
weigh every flower 0.5. The white's 20° glide angle is an estimate the table leaves blank, and the comment says
so.

The breeze (grid decay and diffusion, travelling value noise at 0.035/0.1 Hz and 10–40 pt/s, gust fronts of
60–120 pt/s every 20–60 s) and the flowers (damped tip springs with drag ∝ |v|·v and a per-stem phase, layout per
rect bottom, the click-bloom timeline) follow plan Task 1.

The director's correction is built as the brief lists it:
- soft walls one wingspan deep;
- a preferred direction that drifts as a random walk over the species' persistence time;
- a new direction drawn about the wall's normal at a wall (a correlated random walk);
- slow value-noise altitude wander;
- waypoints anywhere in the terrain, area-weighted, 65 % of them in the bottom 40 %.

Nothing here is unneeded. The behaviour fields (nectar, bask, flush, host, pupa, share) are there because the
plan asks for "every row" of the table.

No per-step heap allocation in `Butterfly.fly`, `Breeze.step` (the double buffers are swapped) or
`Meadowland.step`. Only a click allocates (a small dictionary in `bloom`). The per-step cost is negligible.

**The paths picture, looked at:** the flight is not clustered in the middle any more.
- On the 1800 × 1169 field the tracks span about 85–95 % of the width and 60–85 % of the height.
- Each species leaves an empty border about one wingspan deep on every side, which is the spec'd soft wall.
- The monarch shows long straight glides, the white a dense zig-zag, the morpho kinked bounds, the swallowtail
  wide floating loops.

Over 30 random starts × 180 s per species:
- the bottom 40 % holds 52–62 % of flight time, as asked;
- the top third holds only 11–14 %;
- in the single-seed 90 s picture the white never enters the top third and the morpho barely does.

So "roams the whole field" holds on laptop and monitor sizes, biased low. It fails in two places: tiny viewports
(finding 2) and the flower band itself (finding 3).

Re-checked at `meadow-t1-2` @ 1e11a1b (fix commits 4b8ad72..1e11a1b on 0cce823), blind to `reports/t1-2.md`. The
check worktree ran `swift run -c release OceanChecks` → `200 passed, 0 failed` (195 before; the 5 new Meadow
checks all pass). I took my own numbers below from a throwaway probe in the check worktree; it was removed and nothing was

**(a) The white's per-beat surge (σ 0.45): faithful, though its value was tuned.** The research (§0.3, §1.1)
gives the white's mean tangential acceleration as 8.5 m/s², about the same as its normal 7.6. §1.4's force model
has no term for it except the clap lurch. The surge adds that missing beat-to-beat speeding up and slowing down,
and it applies to the white only, as the research's signature table singles out the white. The code comment admits
0.45 was picked to land the ratio. Checked against the research's own scale, its size holds:
- Made dimensionless per beat, the research's figures give a_t/(v·f) = 8.5/(2.37 × 11.8) = 0.30 and
  a_n/(v·f) = 7.6/(2.37 × 11.8) = 0.27.
- The sim gives 0.23 and 0.19 (5 seeds × 120 s, per beat).
- Both are about 0.73 × the measured values, with no overshoot, so the surge isn't inflated to pass the check.
- Without the surge, a_t would be almost nothing at the beat scale: a no-jink white still shows 0.215.

A caveat for the manager's judgement, not a finding: the comment cites "2.37 ± 0.44 m/s". That is the spread
between flights, not a per-beat measure, and the sim's per-beat speed varies more (CV 0.27 against 0.19). It is
an estimate in the research's sense ("tuned by eye against the numbers"), not a measured value.

**(b) The morpho's exemption from "sinks with frozen wings": a true fact about the model, but broader than it
needs to be.**
- With beats off the morpho's bound never flips, so its wanted heading stays tilted ±22°.
- §1.4's always-on drag toward the wanted airspeed then carries it up the tilt.
- Probe (θ = π, 20 seeds × 1 s): with a dropping bound it sinks 110–124 pt; with a climbing one 0–14 pt. It never
  climbs. The other three sink 60–130 pt on every seed.

So "> 30 pt" can't hold for a climbing-bound morpho, and the exemption is not a loosening of anything that
existed: the sink part is new in this round. The morpho's lift timing is still fully checked by the upstroke
≡ no-lift part and the downstroke rise part. Gravity is one shared line (`Butterfly.swift`, `ay = sp.gravity *
scale`), so the other three species prove it. A tighter form would still hold the morpho to "sink ≥ 0" instead of
skipping it; that is the manager's call, not a blocker.
