# Review s1: the Meadow sketch before the Swift port

Briefs: `briefs/s1.md` and `briefs/s1r2.md` through `briefs/s1r6.md`

Reviewed commit: `3f02cb3607998ba7d9a82c18da2b0b823dd091c1`

All source line numbers refer to `Tools/meadow-sketch/meadow.html` at that commit.

## Design

Use the sketch as the visual and motion reference. Resolve the population and pointer transitions before using the
JavaScript as the behavioral reference. The separate simulation and character-grid painter give the Swift port a
clear split. The accepted motion comes from the combination of wingbeat, thrust, drag, turns and height. Copying the
slider multipliers alone will miss that interaction.

The fresh defaults and all 46 individual ↺ buttons match the director's settings on the board, including 7
butterflies, all four species, Glide 0.10 and Leaves 0.90. In four 180-second runs with no pointer input, the current
defaults spent 74.7% of butterfly time in flight or approach, versus 74.1% in round 2 at the director's prior
settings. Round 6 at those same prior settings spent 75.9% in the air. Glides still occurred at 0.10 in every seed
and occupied 2.6% of all butterfly samples.

Paper uses distinct species pigments and readable ink markings. The cabbage white and daisy remain visible in the
matched seed-42 stills. Comparing Depth 0, 1 and 2 shows the flowers and leaves receding and a rim or paper halo
appearing around high butterflies as Depth rises. Calm softened both themes. The grid overlay keeps its opacity. After fonts settled, all six dark canvas
captures matched round 5 (`b01db3f`) byte for byte across Depth 0/1/2 with Calm off and on.

## What is missing?

- A port fixture that records a seed, the normalized settings, viewport and cell size, a timed pointer trace, and
  expected state and canvas samples at fixed ticks. Copy settings is useful for sharing a preset. It omits the
  generated seed, viewport and input history needed to replay a scene.
- A defined clock and unit mapping for the port. At 1× the core uses seconds and a nominal 1/60 s step. Heading and
  flower springs use semi-implicit Euler, thrust and downwash integrate with `dt`, while jink, bound and glide-bout
  decisions count wingbeats. The sketch also uses CSS pixels scaled by `cw / 13`, and pointer events arrive in wall
  time. Preserve those distinctions and make a deliberate choice about the sketch's 7.5 Hz wingbeat cap, its
  eight-step catch-up policy and the app's draw cadence.
- Boundary semantics for a butterfly that enters on a small display, exits while large, or is landing when its
  display or target moves. The sketch provides one rectangle. The Swift port also needs the actual display terrain,
  including gaps, and an explicit rule for existing perches and clicked flowers when displays change.

### Check evidence

- Ran headless Chrome 158 via Playwright against a detached copy. For the long runs, drove the sketch's own 1/60 s
  update for 10,800 steps per case and sampled every sixth step. The 2560 × 1440 runs at seeds 1, 17, 42 and 2026
  held seven butterflies throughout, with 28–41 planted flowers and peak dust of 25–32. No stale flower
  reservations or non-finite sampled state/cell values appeared in those runs. The 340 × 150 area-count run at seed 1
  stayed finite with one butterfly and one flower. Ordinary runs reported zero browser errors. The malformed
  species-storage case produced the startup exception in finding 3.
- Sent browser pointer moves, holds and clicks. A center hold recruited one butterfly, a later hold settled it on the
  pointer, moving released it, and a left-edge hold settled one in view. Thirty fast and thirty spaced clicks at one
  spot kept a single clicked flower. Touching the occupied opening bloom at seed 42 released its visit reservation, sent the butterfly into flight,
  and emitted 15 particles. Ordinary play aged out old pollen and fading flowers. Normal count changes at full size settled
  at 12 and then 1 within 15 simulated seconds. All species Off drained the active population.
- Exercised each control with the DOM input or chip handler, verified storage, clicked its own reset and audited the
  runtime use against the hint. Wingbeat, lurch, zig-zag, gliding, travel speed, turning, bob, height and ease feed the
  phase/flight model. Hunger, warmth, sensing, touch-and-go, boldness and relay feed decisions and approach
  transitions. Downwash and nod affect the air field and flower spring. Density, Leaves, Breeze, Shadows,
  Green, Grass, Depth, Sparkle, Theme, Calm and Grid lines reach their respective scene or painter paths. The
  clipboard read back the full live settings object plus the sketch label. A regular edited preset survived reload
  unchanged and syntactically invalid storage fell back to defaults.
- With the unmodified page running real animation frames, seed 42 at 2560 × 1440 reported 7.4 ms average render
  time in dark at DPR 1 and 7.3 ms on paper with Calm at DPR 2. Each had a 16.7 ms p95 interval over 180
  frames. After 100 pointer clicks the seed-42 dark run had 44 flowers and a 9.9 ms render label with the same
  16.7 ms p95 interval. These are headless browser frame timings. The label measures CPU time around the render
  call and excludes the simulation step and compositor.
